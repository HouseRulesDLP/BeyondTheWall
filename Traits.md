# Character Traits

Characters get their first trait at level 2 and additional traits are learned at levels 6, 10 & 14.  This is a change from the core rules which grant traits at levels 1, 5 & 9.  This was done to spread out the levels at which character gain upgrades rather than overloading the odd levels as the core rules do.

The traits from the core rules work largely as given.  Only new and changed traits are listed in this document.

Unless otherwise specified, each trait may only be taken once.

## General Traits

### Changed Traits

#### Fast Healer
You roll an additional Hit Die when rolling for recovered Hit Points after your character takes a [Short](Rest_and_Recovery.md#short%20rest) or [Long](Rest_and_Recovery.md#long%20rest) Rest.

#### Great Bravery
While this character certainly becomes frightened, he will not back down because of fear. He is immune to the [Frightened](Conditions.md#frightened) condition.

#### Linguist
Due to study and a gift for [languages](Languages.md), this character knows three additional languages (one of which may be rare).  This trait may be taken multiple times, gaining three new languages each time.

#### Reserves of Strength
This ability has been replaced by [Second Wind](#second-wind).

#### Unassuming
Your character has an uncanny knack for escaping notice. At the beginning of any encounter he may spend a Fortune Point to be automatically hidden without any roll, provided that the surroundings would allow it.

#### Very Helpful
When this character helps another, double the bonus this character grants to the ability check.

### New Traits

#### Actor
Your character is skilled at mimicry and dramatics. They gain +2 on any [deception](Skills.md#deception) checks to pretend to be someone they are not. They can also mimic accents and speech patterns.

#### Alert
Your character is always on the lookout for danger.  They gain +1 Initiative and have +2 on perception rolls to avoid being surprised.

#### Always Prepared
Your character often has unusual but helpful objects at hand. He may spend a Fortune Point to have any item he might have reasonably procured along the way and which could fit in his bag.

#### Animal Companion
You gain a +2 bonus on all [reaction rolls](Reactions.md#reaction%20rolls) involving natural, fae or giant animals. You may take one as an [Ally](Hirelings_and_Allies.md#allies) so long as it's HD do not exceed your level. If you start play with this Trait, you may select a normal animal to be your companion at the start of play (so long as it complies with the above restrictions). As you grow in power, so to will your companion, gaining their own experience in the same manner as any other Ally. It is up to the GM to adjudicate when they have amassed enough XP to gain a new HD, but typically aggressive (or trained in combat) animals should level up as a fighter while more docile animals should level up as a Rogue. Upon gaining additional HD, the beast may gain additional bonuses to hit and on saving throws (similar to other beasts of similar HD). It is up to the GM whether any additional abilities may be gained.

#### Armor Training
Your character understands how to move in a particular weight of armor effectively (Light, Medium or Heavy; see [Armor](CoinAndItsUses.md#Armor) in the Equipment document).  They must have light armor training before they can take medium.  They must have medium armor training before they can take heavy. Without this Trait (or equivalent Class Ability), a character suffers *Disadvantage* on any [Physical Checks](Glossary.md#physical%20check) while wearing armor they have not been trained for.

#### Catch Your Breath
You can regain 1 level of [Exhaustion](Conditions.md#exhaustion) with a [Short Rest](Rest_and_Recovery.md#short%20rest).

#### Contortionist
Your character makes rolls to escape restraints with *Advantage*.

#### Die Hard
Your [death countdown pool](Countdown_Pools.md#death%20pool) is made up of d6s instead of d4s.

#### Great Fortune
Your starting and max Fortune Point values increase by 1 each.  You may take this trait twice.

#### Great Leader
Like the heroes of past ages, your character character commands respect by their bearing alone. By spending a Fortune Point, they can give all of their companions within near or close range who can see and hear them +2 to all of their rolls during the next Round.

#### Healer
Your character is a gifted practitioner of medicine. When caring for someone who is healing, their rate of HP recovery is increased by a number of HP equal to the patient's HD. The patient also has *Advantage* on rolls to recover from disease, poison and [lingering injuries](Lingering_Injury.md). Additionally, she has *Advantage* when attempting to stabilize a dying character.

#### Leader
[Allies and Hirelings](Hirelings_and_Allies.md) under your command gain +1 to Morale and Loyalty so long as they can see and hear you.

#### Merchant's Eye
Your character has a gift for appraising objects of various sorts. Under ordinary circumstances, he automatically knows the approximate value of any item. If a roll is deemed necessary, the character makes the roll with *Advantage*.

#### Second Wind
A brief moment’s respite is all this character needs to be ready to get back into the fight. This character may spend a Fortune Point and take 1 minute to gain the benefits of a [short rest](Rest_and_Recovery.md#short%20rest).  This does not count against their daily short rest limit.

#### Self Improvement
Raise one of your character’s ability scores by 2 points, or two ability scores by 1 point each.  No ability score may be raised above 18 in this fashion.  This trait may be taken multiple times.

#### Superior Training
Take an additional Knack for your class.

#### Survivalist
Your character is at home in the wilds. She can always find enough food and water for herself, though still must make [foraging](Wilderness.md#foraging) or [hunting](Wilderness.md#hunting) tests to feed others. This trait has no effect when in the harshest of conditions, such as a scalding desert or a Dark Lord’s blighted domain.

Additionally, she is skilled in activities such as tracking, animal trapping, fishing and so forth.

#### Tenacity
Some heroes don’t know when to quit and will stay in the fight against all odds. Your character gains an additional Hit Point per level, including any past levels if this Trait is chosen later in the character’s career.

#### Weapon Training
This character gains proficiency with an additional weapon group (light, medium or heavy).  This trait may be taken multiple times.

## Alignment Traits
These are actually renamed as [Allegiance](Cosmic_Allegiance.md) Traits.

### Changed Traits

#### Breaker
When this trait is taken, a specific [Allegiance](Cosmic_Allegiance.md) is named and the character cannot be of the named Allegiance. Their blows are filled with the power of their cosmic patron and thus grant +2 to hit and damage against creatures of the named Allegiance and also count as magical for resistance and immunity purposes. After scoring a hit against a creature of the named Allegiance, this character may spend a Fortune Point to have their attack automatically do maximum damage. This trait may be taken multiple times, applying to a different Allegiance each time.

#### Defender of the Balance
Replaced by [Warded](#warded).

#### Eternal Hero
removed

#### Mercurial Will
Replaced by [Rebuke](#rebuke).

#### Resistant to Magic
Replaced by [Warded](#warded).

#### Summoner
The character may cast the Summoning spell to summon supernatural creatures as a mage of the same level and may use whichever ability score is highest of Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma when doing so.  The creature summoned must be of the same [Allegiance](Cosmic_Allegiance.md) as the character.

#### Touched by Chaos
Removed

#### Warrior of Law
Replaced by [Breaker](#breaker).

### New Traits

#### Rebuke
When this trait is taken, a specific [Allegiance](Cosmic_Allegiance.md) is named and your character cannot be of the named Allegiance.  Your character’s very presence is an anathema to supernatural creatures of the named Allegiance. She may use her action to attempt to drive off supernatural creatures of the named Allegiance. She makes a Charisma Check, adding in her Character Level as a bonus. Any creatures able to see and hear her must make a Will Saving Throw using the check's total as the *Difficulty*. If the creature's save fails, it becomes [Frightened](Conditions.md#frightened) of your character for the next minute. If the Charisma check was also a critical success, those creature are [Routed](Conditions.md#routed) for the next 24 hours. Any creature who passes their Will Save becomes immune to this ability from your character for the next 24 hours, unless you spend a Fortune Point. This trait may be taken multiple times, applying to a different Allegiance each time.

#### Warded
When this trait is taken, a specific [Allegiance](Cosmic_Allegiance.md) is named and your character cannot be of the named Allegiance.  Your character has *Advantage* on all saving throws to defend against the supernatural powers of creatures of the named Allegiance.  If such a saving throw is failed, you may spend a Fortune Point to turn that failure into a success.  This trait may be taken multiple times, applying to a different Allegiance each time.

## Combat Traits

### Changed Traits

#### Combat Mastery
This character’s skill at arms is unmatched. If her level is greater than the hit dice of her strongest opponent, she gains +2 on all to-hit rolls and +2 armor class.

#### Distracting Fighter
removed

#### Favored Enemy
removed

#### Fighting Finesse
removed

#### Mighty Shot
removed

#### Shield Fighter
removed

### New Traits

#### Blind Fighting
The [Blinded](Conditions.md#blinded) condition does not negatively affect your character when making melee attacks at Close range.

#### Knife Fighting
When your character is armed with two knives/daggers, he receives a +1 bonus to hit, a +1 bonus to AC, and does 2d4p/s damage in melee combat.

#### Mage Slayer
If a creature within melee reach casts a spell, this character may use their reaction to make a melee attack against it.  When a creature must make a concentration roll due to damage caused by this character, it does so with disadvantage.

#### Off-Hand Proficiency
This character may wield a medium one-handed weapon in their off-hand at the same time as they wield a medium one-handed weapon in their primary hand.  While wielding two weapons, this character gains a +1 bonus to both AC and to hit and may attack with either weapon.  See [Fighting with Two Weapons](README.md#fighting%20with%20two%20weapons) for the normal rules.

## Spellcasting Traits

### Changed Traits

#### Flexible Ritualist
removed

#### Potent Magic
This character picks two spells that she already knows.  Saving Throws to resist these spells are rolled with *Disadvantage*.  This trait may be taken multiple times, applying to different spells each time.

#### Skilled Caster
After a great deal of practice, your character has mastered a particular type of magic. Choose two  spells known by the character.  When casting these spells, you roll d8s instead of d6s for your Invocation Dice..  This trait may be taken multiple times, applying to two different spells each time.

#### Touched by Magic
Some have a wild, untrained gift for Magic; others learn just a bit and then do not continue their studies.  Pick two 1st levels spells from the same [Tradition](Magic.md#traditions).  Your character can now cast them.  She has a number of Invocation Dice with which to invoke these spells equal to the highest of her Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma Modifiers. These spells are always cast as if by a 1st level spellcaster.  This trait may be taken multiple times to learn additional magic but no additional Invocation Dice are gained.

This trait does nothing for Mages and hybrid Mages.

#### Ultimate Enchantment
removed

#### War Wizard
Through martial training and practice, your mage has learned how best to harness the destructive powers of magic. He may add his highest [Mental Ability](Glossary.md#mental%20abilities) modifier to the damage of any spell he casts.  Additionally, [concentration](Magic.md#concentration) checks are made with *Advantage*.

### New Traits

#### By Will Alone
Pick a single spell your character can cast.  When casting this spell, she may forgo the Somatic and Verbal [components](Magic.md#components) of the spell.  Any Material component requirements must still be met.  This trait may be taken multiple times, applying to a different spell each time.

#### Supreme Concentration
Your character may [concentrate](Magic.md#concentration) on 2 spells at the same time.  If an event would potentially break her concentration, she must make a concentration check for each spell.

#### Well of Power
Your mage may spend a Fortune Point to regain a number of expended [Invocation Dice](Magic.md#invocation%20dice) equal to his highest [Mental](Glossary.md#mental%20abilities) ability modifier upon completing a [Short Rest](Rest_and_Recovery.md#short%20rest).

## Supernatural Traits

### Changed Traits

#### Child of the Sea
replaced by [Wilderness Friend](#wilderness-friend).

#### Earth Caller
replaced by [Elemental Caller](#elemental-caller).

#### Insight
replaced by [Soul Gaze](#soul-gaze)

#### King's Touch
replaced by [Healing Touch](#healing-touch)

#### Oathkeeper
When your character witnesses an oath or makes one herself, fate binds the oath taker to see it done. Anyone who makes an oath in your character’s presence, including the character herself, must keep their word or find themselves halving all healing received and unable to regain Fortune Points unless they are working towards fulfilling their oath.

#### Serpent's Speech
replaced by [Beast Speech](#beast-speech)

#### Shapechanger
As given in the core rules with the following exceptions:  This change lasts for 1 hour per character level. Changing back earlier requires a Will saving throw or the expenditure of a Fortune Point.

#### Spirit Friend
For some reason, spirits are naturally disposed to like or obey your character. Perhaps he is more real to them than other physical beings, or has a mystical heritage of some sort. He gains *Advantage* on all [social checks](Glossary.md#social%20check) involving disembodied spirits and on all rolls to summon or bind them.

### New Traits

#### Beast Speech
Whether because of heritage, a mystical pact, or an unknown gift, your character has a great affinity with a particular type of animal (felines, canines, serpents, birds, etc). She learns their [language](Languages.md), has a rough sense of when they are nearby, and gains a +2 on any [Social Checks](Glossary.md#social%20check) involving them.  This trait may be taken multiple times, applying to a different animal type each time.

#### Cat's Eyes
Your character gains the Low-Light Vision trait if he does not already have it. If he does, he gains the Darkvision trait. If he already has Darkvision, this trait does nothing for him.

#### Elemental Caller
Select a particular elemental type (earth, air, fire or water) when this trait is selected.  Due to bloodline, oath or some other reason, all manifested or aware elementals of the named type in Near range of your character must present themselves; socially if they are capable of speech, or wordlessly if they are not. Your character can perceive them even if they are invisible or inaudible.  He gains the appropriate elemental [language](Languages.md) as a bonus language.  This trait may be taken multiple times, applying to a different elemental type each time.

#### Fae Touched
Your character has been exposed to the magic of Faerie or has fae blood running in her veins.  She gains a +2 bonus to any Saving Throw vs magic.  Additionally, after reaching adulthood, her aging slows to half the normal rate for her species.  Characters that are already Fae Blooded (such as Elves and Goblins) cannot take this trait.

#### Healing Touch
Your character’s hands have the power to heal the wounded. He may lay hands on another and grant them the same benefits as a [Short Rest](Rest_and_Recovery.md#short%20rest) (though this does not count against the number of short rests they may take in a day).  He may use this ability a number of times per day equal to his highest [Mental Ability](Glossary.md#mental%20abilities) modifier.  Upon completing a [Long Rest](Rest_and_Recovery.md#long%20rest), one use of this ability are regained.  This trait may be taken multiple times, each time gaining one additional use of this ability.

#### Infuse Weapon
This character may infuse part of her soul into her weapon attacks (including unarmed).  When she does so, her attacks are treated as magical for the next minute, after which she gains 1 level of [Exhaustion](Conditions.md#exhaustion).

#### Second Sight
This character gains the Sense Magic ability as if they were a [Mage](classes/Mage.md#sense%20magic).

#### Soul Gaze
This character has the frightening ability to look into another’s soul. When this character first encounters any creature with a True Name, she may spend a Fortune Point to learn the creature’s [Allegience](Cosmic_Allegiance.md) and [Type](Bestiary.md#type).  Additionally, she may make a Charisma check for a chance that the creature’s True Name will come to her that night in a dream.  The difficulty of the check is 10 + 1 per HD or Level of the target.

#### Wilderness Friend
Pick a terrain type (such as forest, desert, ocean, mountain, etc).  This character has *Advantage* on [Survival](Skills.md#survival) checks in this terrain.  Additionally, sometimes the natural spirits of the land in this terrain whisper to her and she gains +2 on any Charisma roll to interact with such spirits.  This trait may be taken multiple times, applying to a different terrain type each time.
