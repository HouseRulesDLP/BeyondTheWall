# Glossary

## Ability Check
A [D20 Check](#D20%20Check) to which an [Ability Modifier](#Ability%20Modifier) is added to the D20 roll.

## Ability Modifier
A value used to modify various die rolls (typically [Ability](#Ability%20Check) and [Skilled](#Skilled%20Check) checks).  This value is derived from the character's Ability Score.

## Advantage
When rolling the dice, roll twice and take the **better** result.

## Armor Class
A measure of how hard it is to **damage** a creature.  This is an abstraction of both being difficult to hit in the first place as well as being highly resistant to damage.

## Caster Level
For [Mages](classes/Mage.md), this is equal to their [Character Level](#Character%20Level).  For [hybrid casters](#hybrid%20class), this is generally equal to 1/3 or 2/3 their [Character Level](#Character%20Level).

## Character Level
A measure of a character's overall proficiency with their class.  Range extends from 0 to 15.

## Combat Round
All the [Combat Turns](#Combat%20Turn) of all participating creatures.  Approximately 10 seconds of time.

## Combat Turn
A specific individual's turn to act during a [Combat Round](#Combat%20Round).

## Concealed Door
A type of [Hidden Door](#Hidden%20Door) that is simply obscured behind some opaque object (like a curtain).

## Concentration
This is the act of concentrating on a spell (or other activity that requires intense concentration).  Concentration may be lost by the character taking damage or other possible distractions.  See [Concentrating](Conditions.md#Concentrating) in the Conditions chapter.

## Concentration Check
A check to maintain [Concentration](Conditions.md#concentration).  Typically this takes the form of a Wisdom Mod+Hold Saving Throw.

## Condition
A state that (typically) negatively effects a given character.  See the [Conditions](Conditions.md) chapter.

## Contested Check
When two or more individuals are directly opposing each other in a check, a Contested Check may be made.  All parties involved roll their respective checks.  Whoever gets the highest check total is the winner.  If a tie results and does not make sense, re-roll.

## Core Class
One of the three core (aka single) classes: [Warrior](Warrior.md), [Rogue](Rogue.md) and [Mage](Mage.md).

## Critical Hit
A natural 20 on the attack roll that *exceeds* (not matches) the target's [Armor Class](#Armor%20Class).  On a critical hit, the number of damage *dice* (but not static bonuses) is doubled.

## D20 Check
Any check that involves rolling a D20 plus modifiers and comparing the total to a [Task Difficulty](README.md#Task%20Difficulty).

## Damage Immunity
If a target is immune to the type of damage being done, it takes 0 damage.

## Damage Resistance
If a target is resistant to the type of damage being done, it takes only half the damage (rounded down), but still takes a minimum of 1.

## Darkvision
Allows the creature to see in either dim light or full darkness.  Dim lighting is treated as bright light and is in full color.  Full darkness is treated as dim lighting and is only in shades of grey.  Does not work against magical darkness.

## Die Steps
d2 -> d3 -> d4 -> d5 -> d6 -> d8 -> d10 -> d12 -> d16 -> d20 -> d24 -> d30

## Disadvantage
When rolling the dice, roll twice and take the _worse_ result.

## Exhausted
A [Condition](Conditions.md) representing the character fatiguing and becoming less and less capable the more tired they get.  See [Exhaustion](Conditions.md#Exhaustion).

## Fighting Withdrawal
Moving out of melee combat while still maintaining your defense.  Splitting your concentration like this means you can only retreat at your normal move, not [Run](#Run) or [Sprint](#Sprint).  See also [Parting Shot](#parting%20shot).

## Fortune Points
A pool of points used to grant characters certain bonuses as a reward for heroic and characterful role-play.

## Full Class
See [Core Class](#Core%20Class).

## Hidden Door
A door obscured from sight that must be found before it can be used.  See also [Concealed](#concealed%20door) and [Secret](#Secret%20Door) doors.

## Hit Die
A die used in rolling for [Hit Points](#Hit%20Points).

## Hit Points
A score indicating how difficult it is to kill a target.

## Hybrid Class
A class that blends the features of two other classes: [Rogue-Mage](Rogue-Mage.md), [Warrior-Mage](Warrior-Mage.md) and [Warrior-Rogue](Warrior-Rogue.md).

## Invocation Roll
When casting a spell, a caster must roll one or more of their [Invocation Dice](Magic.md#The%20Invocation%20Dice%20Pool) in an attempt to meet or exceed the spell's [Difficulty](Magic.md#Spell%20Difficulty).

## Knack
A special ability selected from a list of such abilities available to certain classes.

## Low-Light Vision
Allows a creature to see in dim lighting conditions as if it were bright light, but the vision is in shades of grey only. Full darkness still prevents any sight.

## Magical Focus
A device used by mages to aid in the casting of their spells.  See [Spellcasting Focus](Mage.md#Spellcasting%20Focus).

## Mental Abilities
*Intelligence*, *Wisdom* and *Charisma*

## Mental Check
Any [Ability Check](#Ability%20Check) involving *Intelligence*, *Wisdom* or *Charisma*.

## Moral
- [ ] Definition of morale.

## Natural Roll
The die face that is showing after the roll, without applying any modifiers.

## Opposed Check
Any check in which two or more actors are competing directly against one another.  Resolved by both sides rolling their respective Skill or Ability checks with the highest total being the winner.  If a tie does not make sense, re-roll.

## Parting Shot
When someone in melee combat attempts to leave combat without making a [Fighting Withdrawal](#fighting%20withdrawal), all enemies in melee range get to make a single free attack against the fleeing party with a +2 bonus to hit.

## Perception Check
A type of [[#Skilled Check]] involving one of the actor's senses (typically sight or hearing) based on *Wisdom*.  See [Perception](Skills.md#Perception).

## Physical Abilities
*Strength*, *Dexterity* and *Constitution*

## Physical Check
Any [Ability Check](#Ability%20Check) involving *Strength*, *Dexterity* or *Constitution*.

## Run
Move at double your normal movement, but suffer [Disadvantage](#Disadvantage) on all [perception](Skills.md#perception) checks and cannot make attacks.

## Secret Door
A type of [Hidden Door](#Hidden%20Door) that is disguised to be part of the environment.

## Sigil
A symbolic representation of a magical spell.  Such symbols can make spellcasting easier for a mage.  See [[Magic.md#Sigils]] for more details.

## Skill Bonus
A bonus that is added to the D20 roll when performing a [Skilled Check](#Skilled%20Check).  The value of this bonus is dependent on the creature's [Rank](Skills.md#Skill%20Ranks) with the skill in question.

## Skilled Check
An [Ability Check](#Ability%20Check) to which a creature's [Skill Bonus](#Skill%20Bonus) may be added.

## Social Check
A type of D20 Check used in a social interaction (often a [Charisma Check](#Ability%20Check)); such as attempting to persuade a guard to let you pass, bartering for a better deal on a horse or convincing a judge that you are innocent of the crime to which you are accused.  See [Deception](Skills.md#Deception), [Insight](Skills.md#Insight), [Intimidation](Skills.md#Intimidation) and [Persuasion](Skills.md#Persuasion).

## Spell Difficulty
The target value to meet or exceed on a mage's [Invocation Roll](#Invocation%20Roll) in order to successfully cast a spell. See [Spell Difficulty](Magic.md#Spell%20Difficulty) in the Magic document for more details.

## Spell Tier
A value indicating how powerful a spell is.  Determines the base [Spell Difficulty](#Spell%20Difficulty).

## Sprint
Move at triple your normal movement, but suffer the same penalties as [Running](#run), and attacks against you have [Advantage](#Advantage).

## Task Difficulty
The [number](README.md#Task%20Difficulty) that must be met or exceeded when making a [D20 Check](#D20%20Check).  Sometimes abbreviated as "diff" or "TD".

## Time Steps
1 reaction -> 1 action -> 1 combat turn -> 1 round -> 1 minute -> 1 exploration turn (10 minutes) -> 1 hour -> 1 watch (4 hours) -> 1 day -> 1 week -> 1 fortnight (2 weeks) -> 1 month -> 1 season (3 months) -> 1 year -> x2 years for each additional step

## Trait
A special ability available to all classes at certain level ups.  See the [Traits](Traits.md) chapter.

## True Name
Creatures with supernatural powers (including spellcasting) or of otherworldly origins all have True Names.  This is a secret name that ties the being to the supernatural world, but may be used against them if it is learned by others.
