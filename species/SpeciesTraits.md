# Species Traits

## %%Autumn Folk%%
**Autumn Folk:** The age of the elves is long since past. A Elf’s Base and Max [Fortune Points](Glossary.md#Fortune%20Points) are reduced by 1 (to a minimum 0 Base Fortune and 1 Max Fortune).

## %%Dust of Ages%%
**Dust of Ages:** The age of the dwarf is long since past. A Dwarf’s Base and Max [Fortune Points](Glossary.md#Fortune%20Points) are reduced by 1 (to a minimum 0 Base Fortune and 1 Max Fortune).

## %%Enduring as Stone%%
**Enduring as Stone:** Dwarves have endurance beyond that of humans.  They gain +1 HP per level and roll [Hit Dice](Glossary.md#Hit%20Die) with [Advantage](Glossary.md#Advantage) when healing.

## %%Fae Blooded%%
**Fae Blooded:** As a fae being, your inherently magical nature grants you [Advantage](Glossary.md#Advantage) on all Saving Throws vs magic.

## %%Fortitude of Iron%%
**Fortitude of Iron:** Dwarves have [Advantage](Glossary.md#Advantage) on saving throws vs Poison and Disease.

## %%Giant Strength%%
**Giant Strength:** You increase your Strength by +4 for determining the size of your [Readied](CoinAndItsUses.md#Readied%20Items) and [Stowed](CoinAndItsUses.md#Stowed%20Items) gear lists.

## %%Hob Spirit%%
**Hob Spirit:** Hobs raise the spirits of even the most downtrodden and are valued by all their companions. So long as they can see and hear him, all of a Hob's companions within 30 feet gain [Advantage](Glossary.md#Advantage) on saving throws to resist [fear](Conditions.md#Frightened) and despair and NPC allies gain Advantage on [Morale](Hirelings_and_Allies.md#Morale) checks.

## %%Intrepid%%
**Intrepid:** Your Base and Max **Fortune Points** are each increased by 1.

## %%Keen Senses%%
**Keen Senses:** You have [Advantage](Glossary.md#Advantage) on Perception checks.

## %%Low-Light Vision%%
**Low-Light Vision:** You see in dim light nearly as well as full daylight; however, this vision is shades of gray only, so color cannot be discerned in these conditions.  Also, this ability does not function in full darkness.

## %%Massive%%
**Massive:** You treat two-handed melee weapons as if they were [Versatile](CoinAndItsUses.md#Weapon%20Properties) and Versatile weapons as if they were one-handed (while still gaining [Advantage](Glossary.md#Advantage) on damage rolls) and you can wield [Medium](CoinAndItsUses.md#Weight) weapons in your off-hand as if they were Light. However, your large hands wield actual [Light](CoinAndItsUses.md#Weight) weapons with [Disadvantage](Glossary.md#Disadvantage) and any armor built for your size costs 4 times the normal amount. Finally, you must eat and drink 4 times as much as a human.

## %%Relentless Endurance%%
**Relentless Endurance:** You do not tire easily and have [Advantage](Glossary.md#Advantage) on any Saving Throw or Ability Check to resist gaining a level of [Exhaustion](Conditions.md#Exhaustion).  

## %%Small Stature%%
**Small Stature:** Being small hurts in a fight. You are a small creature and have [Disadvantage](Glossary.md#Disadvantage) wielding any weapon with the [Heavy](CoinAndItsUses.md#Weight) property, or when wielding a [Versatile](CoinAndItsUses.md#Weapon%20Properties) weapon one-handed.

On the other hand, you gain [Advantage](Glossary.md#Advantage) on all Stealth checks.

## %%Strong Back%%
**Strong Back:** You increase your Strength by +2 for determining the size of your [Readied](CoinAndItsUses.md#Readied%20Items) and [Stowed](CoinAndItsUses.md#Stowed%20Items) gear lists.

## %%True Name%%
**True Name:** As a fae being, you have a [True Name](Glossary.md#True%20Name), and those with the knowledge may use it against you. By calling out your True Name, a creature gains [Advantage](Glossary.md#Advantage) on all actions taken against you, including attacks and saving throws.
