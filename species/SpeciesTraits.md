# Species Traits

## %%Dust of Ages%%
**Dust of Ages:** The age of the dwarf is long since past. A Dwarf’s Base and Max [Fortune Points](Glossary.md#Fortune%20Points) are reduced by 1 (to a minimum 0 Base Fortune and 1 Max Fortune).

## %%Enduring as Stone%%
**Enduring as Stone:** Dwarves have endurance beyond that of humans.  They gain +1 HP per level and roll [Hit Dice](Glossary.md#Hit%20Die) with [Advantage](Glossary.md#Advantage) when healing.

## %%Fae Blooded%%
**Fae Blooded:** As a fae being, your inherently magical nature grants you [Advantage](Glossary.md#Advantage) on all Saving Throws vs magic.

## %%Fortitude of Iron%%
**Fortitude of Iron:** Dwarves have [Advantage](Glossary.md#Advantage) on saving throws vs Poison and Disease.

## %%Low-Light Vision%%
**Low-Light Vision:** You see in dim light nearly as well as full daylight; however, this vision is shades of gray only, so color cannot be discerned in these conditions.  Also, this ability does not function in full darkness.

## %%Relentless Endurance%%
You do not tire easily and have [Advantage](Glossary.md#Advantage) on any Saving Throw or Ability Check to resist gaining a level of [Exhaustion](Conditions.md#Exhaustion).  You also increase your Strength by +2 for determining the size of their [Readied](CoinAndItsUses.md#Readied%20Items) and [Stowed](CoinAndItsUses.md#Stowed%20Items) gear lists.

## %%Small Stature%%
**Small Stature:** Being small hurts in a fight. You are a small creature and have [Disadvantage](Glossary.md#Disadvantage) wielding any weapon with the Heavy [property](CoinAndItsUses.md#Weapon%20Properties), or when wielding a Versatile weapon one-handed.

On the other hand, you gain [Advantage](Glossary.md#Advantage) on all Stealth checks.

## %%True Name%%
**True Name:** As a fae being, you have a [True Name](Glossary.md#True%20Name), and those with the knowledge may use it against you. By calling out your True Name, a creature gains [Advantage](Glossary.md#Advantage) on all actions taken against you, including attacks and saving throws.
