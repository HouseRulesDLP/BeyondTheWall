# Mage
- **Requirements:** INT 11+, WIS 9+
- **HP at Level 1:** 4 + CON Mod
- **HP for Levels 2 to 10:** {Ld4 + CON Mod} per level.
- **HP for Levels 11+:** +1 per level
- **Hit Die:** d4
- **Initiative Bonus:** +0
- **Weapons:** Light & Staff
- **Armor:** Light
