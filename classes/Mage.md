# Mage
- **HP at Level 1:** 4
- **Additional Hit Points (Levels 2 to 10):** d4
- **HP for Levels 11+:** +1/level
- **Hit Die:** d4
- **Initiative Bonus:** +0
- **Weapons:** Light & Staff
- **Armor:** Light
