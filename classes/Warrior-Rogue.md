# Warrior-Rogue
- **HP at Level 1:** 8
- **Hit Dice (for Levels 2 to 10):** d6 (even), d8 (odd)
- **HP for Levels 11+:** +1 (even), +2 (odd)
- **Initiative Bonus:** +1
- **Weapons:** Any
- **Armor:** Light, Medium, Bucklers and Shields
