# Rogue-Mage
- **Requirements:** DEX 11+, INT 11+, WIS 9+
- **HP at Level 1:** 5
- **HP for Levels 2 to 10:** d5
- **HP for Levels 11+:** +1/level
- **Hit Die:** d5
- **Initiative Bonus:** +1
- **Weapons:** Light and Medium
- **Armor:** Light
