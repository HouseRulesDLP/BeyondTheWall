# Rogue-Mage
- **HP at Level 1:** 5
- **Hit Dice (Levels 2 to 10):** d5
- **HP for Levels 11+:** +1/level
- **Initiative Bonus:** +1
- **Weapons:** Light and Medium
- **Armor:** Light

## Class Abilities

### Highly Skilled
Rogue-Mages receive 1 extra Skill at 1st level.  They gain additional skills as shown in the table below.

### Magical Focus
As part of your apprenticeship, you created a unique magical focus to aid in your spell casting. This focus may take most any form (a staff, wand, amulet, holy symbol or similar), but must be easily seen, recognized and hand-held. It also takes up 1 [inventory slot](CoinAndItsUses.md#encumbrance). Mages use these foci to store magical energy.  You can store 1 Invocation Die per Caster Level.  These Invocation Dice remain until used, but when used are automatically expended until the mage recharges the focus by expending his own Invocation Dice on a 1:1 basis.  These Invocation Dice recover normally.

### Read Magic
You know the runes well.  You can decipher magical writing with an Intelligence+[Deduction](Skills.md#deduction) check.  Of course, this requires the time to read the text.

### Sense Magic
Being sensitive to the world of magic, you may determine if a person, place or thing is magical with a Intelligent+[Perception](Skills.md#perception) check. Doing so requires [concentration](Conditions.md#concentrating) and a full minute; so you cannot tell if something is magical simply by being in its presence and people tend to notice when a mage is staring at them.

### Spell Casting 
Spell casting works very different than in the core rules.  Each mage gains a number of Invocation Dice equal to their level plus their Wisdom Modifier.  These dice are used in casting spells.  Spells must be prepared before they are cast.  A mage may not prepare more than the number of spells shown on the table below.  See the [Magic](Magic.md) document for further details.

### True Name
As a magic-user possessing supernatural power, you have a True Name. Those who know your True Name can use it against you. By calling out your True Name, a creature gains *Advantage* on all actions taken against you, including attacks. They also gain *Advantage* on any saving throws to resist your spells or other supernatural abilities.
