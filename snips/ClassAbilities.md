## %%Heroic Stunt%%
Once per turn, when you make a melee attack, you may attempt a Heroic Stunt.  Describe the special effect you wish to add to your attack (such as disarming an opponent, tripping them, pushing them back, etc).  If the attack hits, and the [Natural Roll](README.md#Natural%20Roll) is equal to or greater than your Heroic Stunt target number, the maneuver succeeds.  Note that a Heroic Stunt cannot add to the damage of the attack and the target may be allowed a Saving Throw.

## %%Hauler%%
Add your Constitution Modifier (if positive) when calculating the max sizes of both your [Stowed](CoinAndItsUses.md#stowed%20items) and [Ready](CoinAndItsUses.md#readied%20items) equipment lists.

## %%Minimum Damage%%
Warriors are experts at dealing damage.  If your attack misses the target's AC by less than your level, you still deal your attack's minimum damage to the target.

## %%Weapon Specialization%%
All warriors have a favored weapon with which they are particularly skilled. Pick one weapon at first level. Your character receives a +1 to hit and +{level/2; round up} to damage while wielding that weapon.

## %%Fortune's Favor%%
You add 2 extra Fortune Points to both your Base and Max fortune point limits.

## %%Fortune's Favor, Lesser%%
You add 1 extra Fortune Point to both your Base and Max fortune point limits.

## %%Highly Skilled%%
You receive two additional skill picks at first level. You gain an additional skill pick every odd-numbered level thereafter. Instead of gaining a new skill, you may get better at one you already have, increasing your [Rank](Skills.md#Skill%20Ranks) in that skill.

## %%Magical Focus%%
As part of your apprenticeship, you created a device to aid in casting spells. This focus may take most any form (a staff, wand, amulet or similar), but must be easily seen, recognized and hand-held. It also takes up 1 [inventory slot](CoinAndItsUses.md#Encumbrance). Your focus may be replaced by constructing a new one which takes 1 week in a [Safe Haven](Rest_and_Recovery.md#Safe%20Haven) and 100sp worth of materials.

At each [caster level](Glossary.md#Caster%20Level), you may add a [sigil](Glossary.md#Sigil) into your focus. Each sigil represents a specific spell you have learned (i.e. recorded in your [spell books](Magic.md#Spell%20Books)). Whenever you cast one of these spells, while holding your Spellcasting Focus, you rolls d8s for your [Invocation Dice](Glossary.md#Invocation%20Roll) instead of d6s. It takes 1 Day times the spell's [Tier](Glossary.md#Spell%20Tier) and 50sp per Day to add a new sigil into your focus.

Having a Spellcasting Focus in hand does not count against needing a free hand for spell casting.

## %%Read Magic%%
You know the runes well.  You can decipher magical writing with an Intelligence+[Deduction](Skills.md#deduction) check.  Of course, this requires the time to read the text.

## %%Sense Magic%%
Being sensitive to the world of magic, you may determine if a person, place or thing is magical with a Intelligence Mod+[Perception](Skills.md#perception) check. Doing so requires [concentration](Conditions.md#concentrating) and a full minute; so you cannot tell if something is magical simply by being in its presence and people tend to notice when a mage is staring at them.  You can also use this ability to attempt to decode magical ciphers.

## %%Spell Casting%% 
Spell casting works very different than in the core rules.  Each mage gains a number of [Invocation Dice](Glossary.md#Invocation%20Roll) equal to their [Caster Level](Glossary.md#Caster%20Level) plus their Wisdom Modifier.  These dice are used in casting spells.  Spells must be [prepared](Magic.md#Preparing%20Spells) before they are cast.  A mage may not prepare more than the number of spells shown on the table below.  See the [Magic](Magic.md) document for further details.

## %%True Name%%
As a magic-user possessing supernatural power, you have a [True Name](Glossary.md#True%20Name). Those who know your True Name can use it against you. By calling out your True Name, a creature gains [Advantage](Glossary.md#Advantage) on all actions taken against you, including attacks. They also gain *Advantage* on any saving throws to resist your spells or other supernatural abilities.
